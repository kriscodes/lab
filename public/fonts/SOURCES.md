# Font sources

The publication design self-hosts IBM Plex Sans (400 and 500), Source Serif 4 (400 normal and italic), and IBM Plex Mono (400). Each family’s SIL Open Font License is included alongside its files.

The new Latin WOFF2 faces were obtained from the corresponding `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans` and `@fontsource/source-serif-4` npm packages through jsDelivr on 2026-11-08. Runtime pages request only the committed local files; they make no font-provider requests. License texts were obtained from the Google Fonts repository’s `ofl/ibmplexsans` and `ofl/sourceserif4` directories.

The earlier Instrument Sans assets remain in the repository but are not referenced by the publication design.
